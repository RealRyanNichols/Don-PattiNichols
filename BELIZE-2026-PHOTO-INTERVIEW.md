# Belize, June 2026 — nine photographs, and what only you know

Dad,

Nine of your Belize photos are up on the site now. Until last week every one of them
was labeled the same way — just "Belize, photograph 4." That meant Google couldn't
see them, and anyone using a screen reader got nothing at all.

They've been given real descriptions now, written by looking at them. But those
descriptions only say what's visible in the frame, and then they stop. Nobody knows
which village it was, or who these people are, or what happened five minutes later.
You do.

**A few things before you start:**

Whatever you write goes on the site in your words, exactly as you wrote them. Nothing
gets polished or rewritten.

Fragments are perfect. Three words next to a photo is worth more than a paragraph you
had to work at. "Santa Familia. His name is Marcos." — that's plenty.

Anything you skip just stays the way it is now. Nothing will be made up to fill a gap.
A blank is a blank, and that's fine.

If you'd rather talk it out than write it, call and we'll do it that way.

## 6 — The baptism

*A woman being baptized by immersion in a lagoon, two men supporting her.*

> "A baptism in the lagoon, off the end of the village dock."

This is the most important photograph in the album, and right now the site says one
sentence about it. Take as much room here as you want.

Who is she? Would she want her name told, or would you rather it stay hers?

Who are the two men with her — and who did the baptizing?

Where is this lagoon, and which village?

How did it come about? Was it planned before you got there, or did it happen that week?

Was anything said over her — a verse, a prayer, words you remember?

Who was standing there watching? Family? The whole village?

What was it like for you, being there for it?
